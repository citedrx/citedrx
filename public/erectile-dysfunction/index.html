How we scored these

  1. Evidence Match (4 pts). FDA-approved sildenafil/tadalafil (brand or generic) scores highest since it's the exact product tested in the pivotal trials; compounded or combination-dose products (not individually FDA-reviewed) score lower.
  2. Independent Rating (2 pts). No U.S. News rating exists for this vertical; we scale from the best available third-party data (BBB, Trustpilot), weighted conservatively where sources conflict.
  3. Pricing & Support (2 pts). Transparent flat pricing and easy self-service cancellation score highest; providers with documented billing or cancellation complaints score lower.
  4. Clinical Support Model (1 pt). Standard licensed-clinician intake and review.
  5. Regulatory Standing (1 pt). Full credit by default; deducted for an active FDA warning letter tied to the ED product specifically.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best ED telehealth provider?

Based on CitedRx's scoring, Hims and GoodRx Care score highest because they offer FDA-approved sildenafil or tadalafil (brand or generic) with transparent, flexible pricing. Evidence quality is roughly equal across providers offering the same FDA-approved generic drug: the real differences are pricing transparency and how easy a provider makes it to cancel.

Is generic sildenafil or tadalafil as effective as the brand name?

Yes. FDA-approved generic sildenafil and tadalafil contain the same active ingredient, at the same dose, reviewed by the FDA for bioequivalence to Viagra and Cialis: this is different from compounded ED products, which are custom-mixed by a pharmacy and not individually FDA-approved.

What's the difference between sildenafil (Viagra) and tadalafil (Cialis)?

Both are PDE5 inhibitors with similar efficacy in clinical trials. The practical difference is duration: sildenafil's effective window is roughly 4-5 hours, while tadalafil's half-life of about 17.5 hours gives it an effective window of up to 36 hours, and it also has an FDA-approved once-daily low-dose option.

Is compounded ED medication safe?

Compounded sildenafil/tadalafil products (including flavored chewables and combination doses) are not individually reviewed by the FDA for safety or effectiveness, even when they use FDA-approved active ingredients. In September 2025, the FDA issued a warning letter to BlueChew's parent company over claims that its compounded products contain the "same active ingredients" as brand drugs, finding the framing misleading.

Are PDE5 inhibitors safe for the heart?

A 2024 meta-analysis of 16 studies covering more than 1.2 million men found PDE5 inhibitor use was associated with lower risk of major adverse cardiovascular events and all-cause mortality, not merely a neutral safety profile, though PDE5 inhibitors remain contraindicated with nitrate medications.

How much does ED telehealth cost per month?

Generic sildenafil or tadalafil through telehealth typically runs $20-$65/month depending on dose and provider, often less per-dose with a larger supply. Compounded chewables or combination products can cost more. Brand-name Viagra or Cialis without insurance can run significantly higher.

Are online ED prescriptions legitimate?

Yes, when the provider uses licensed U.S. clinicians for prescribing: telehealth prescribing of PDE5 inhibitors is legal nationwide and these are not controlled substances. Legitimacy still varies by provider on pricing transparency and cancellation practices: check reviews and billing terms before enrolling.
