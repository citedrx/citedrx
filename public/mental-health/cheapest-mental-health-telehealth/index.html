Cheapest Online Therapy & Psychiatry, Priced Low to High

6 of CitedRx's 7 mental health providers publish a price, sorted cheapest first. Each one is shown with its real CitedRx Score right next to the price, because the cheapest option and the best-evidenced option aren't always the same provider.

The short answer: Thriveworks' $0-55 copay range is the lowest if your insurance covers the low end, followed by Rula (~$15/session) and Talkiatry (~$30 copay), all insurance-first models. What this means in plain language

Cheapest and highest-scored aren't in conflict here

Brightside Health is CitedRx's highest-scored mental health provider (9.1/10) and also the cheapest flat self-pay rate on this page ($95/mo for psychiatry-only). That's a different pattern than some categories CitedRx covers, where the cheapest option carries a real evidence tradeoff: here, the lowest-scored providers on this page (Talkiatry, Thriveworks) aren't actually the cheapest.

A copay price only helps if you're in-network

Thriveworks, Rula, and Talkiatry's low numbers assume your specific insurance plan is in-network with that provider. If it isn't, your actual cost could be far higher than the self-pay flat rates from Brightside, BetterHelp, or Talkspace. Confirm your plan's in-network status directly before assuming the lowest number applies to you.

Brightside's $95 is the floor of a tiered structure

$95/mo covers psychiatry (medication management) only; therapy-only runs $299/mo, and combined psychiatry plus therapy runs $349/mo. Don't assume the headline $95 figure covers everything Brightside offers: confirm which tier matches what you actually need.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest online therapy or psychiatry provider?

By published price, Thriveworks' $0-55 copay range is the lowest if your insurance covers the low end, followed by Rula at roughly $15/session with insurance and Talkiatry at roughly $30/session copay. Among flat self-pay monthly rates, Brightside Health's $95/mo psychiatry-only tier is lowest. Cheapest-by-sticker-price isn't the same ranking as CitedRx Score, so check the full comparison before choosing on price alone.

Why do copays and monthly rates appear on the same list?

Insurance-first providers (Rula, Thriveworks, Talkiatry) bill per session as a copay, which depends on your specific plan; self-pay providers (Brightside, BetterHelp, Talkspace) publish a flat monthly rate instead. This page sorts by the lowest published number for comparability, but a copay only applies if the provider is in-network with your plan.

Is the cheapest mental health provider the best one?

Not necessarily. CitedRx's highest-scored provider, Brightside Health (9.1/10), isn't the cheapest by sticker price, and copay-based pricing only helps if a provider is actually in-network with your specific insurance plan. Price is one input; see each provider's full CitedRx Score for the complete picture.
