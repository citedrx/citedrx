Hims Review: Cost, Legitimacy, and What They Actually Prescribe

Hims is CitedRx's top-ranked ED telehealth provider, offering both brand-name and FDA-approved generic sildenafil and tadalafil. Here's what the evidence covers, what it costs, and where it's genuinely not the right fit.

What the trial evidence actually covers

Hims's ED menu is entirely FDA-approved product: generic and brand-name sildenafil and tadalafil, the same molecules with decades of PDE5 inhibitor trial history covered in our research library, including a 2024 meta-analysis of more than 1.2 million men finding PDE5 inhibitor use associated with lower cardiovascular risk, not merely a neutral safety profile. Because Hims doesn't offer a compounded combination product in this category, the trial evidence applies directly to what you'd actually be prescribed, whichever option you choose.

Worth knowing about the company, if not about this specific product: Hims faces separate FDA scrutiny over its compounded weight-loss (GLP-1) marketing. That's an entirely different product line from its ED offering and doesn't affect the evidence picture here.

The genuine tradeoffs

What's genuinely good

  • Both brand-name and FDA-approved generic sildenafil/tadalafil in one place, more choice than most competitors
  • Entirely FDA-approved product for ED: the trial evidence applies directly to what's prescribed
  • Subscription marketed as cancel-anytime, with no long-term commitment
  • One of the largest, most established U.S. telehealth platforms

The real tradeoffs

  • Per-dose billing means your real monthly cost depends on usage: it isn't a predictable flat rate the way GoodRx Care's $18/mo is
  • Self-pay only: no insurance billing, though generics are inexpensive at retail with a GoodRx coupon in some cases
  • No independent third-party rating specific to its ED line comparable to what's available for other CitedRx categories
  • The company carries separate FDA scrutiny over its GLP-1 weight-loss marketing: unrelated to ED, but worth knowing about Hims as a company

Frequently asked questions

Is Hims legit?

Yes. Hims is one of the largest U.S. telehealth platforms, prescribing FDA-approved brand-name and generic sildenafil and tadalafil for ED. It scores 9.1/10 on CitedRx's own evidence-based methodology, currently ranked 1st of 9 ED providers we compare.

How much does Hims cost for ED medication?

Hims's generic sildenafil runs roughly $2-10 per dose depending on strength, and generic tadalafil roughly $15-25 per dose; brand-name Viagra or Cialis cost more. Billing is per-use rather than a flat monthly fee, so your real monthly cost depends on how often you use it. Confirm current pricing directly on Hims's site before enrolling.

Does Hims accept insurance for ED medication?

Hims is self-pay for its ED products rather than insurance-billed. Generic sildenafil and tadalafil are sometimes cheaper through a local pharmacy with a GoodRx coupon if you already have a prescription; compare both before committing.

What does Hims prescribe for ED?

Both FDA-approved generic sildenafil and tadalafil, and brand-name Viagra and Cialis for patients who want the brand specifically. All are the same molecules tested in the pivotal PDE5 inhibitor trials, so the clinical evidence applies directly regardless of which you choose.

Hims vs GoodRx Care: what's the difference?

Hims offers both brand-name and generic sildenafil/tadalafil with per-dose billing; GoodRx Care is generic-only but bundles the visit, medication, and delivery into one flat $18/mo. See our full Hims vs. GoodRx Care comparison for a factor-by-factor breakdown.
