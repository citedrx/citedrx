BlueChew vs. Hims: Chewable Convenience or FDA-Approved Value?

BlueChew's compounded chews cost more per dose than Hims's plain FDA-approved generic, and score far lower on CitedRx's methodology, 4.7/10 versus 9.1/10. Here's the factor-by-factor breakdown, including what the chewable format genuinely offers.

Last reviewed: October 2026 · See all 10 providers →
The short answer: Hims scores far higher than BlueChew (9.1 vs 4.7) and typically costs less per dose, since Hims's plain FDA-approved generic sildenafil or tadalafil runs close to $2/dose against BlueChew's reported $2.65–3.33/chew. BlueChew's products are compounded, not FDA-approved, and its parent company, Dermacare LLC, received an FDA warning letter in September 2025 over marketing language comparing its chews to FDA-approved drugs. Neither company paid for or reviewed this comparison.

What this means in plain language

Chewable isn't automatically a better format, and it costs more here

BlueChew's chewable tablets can be a real convenience if you don't want to swallow a pill, but on a per-dose basis they cost more than Hims's plain FDA-approved sildenafil or tadalafil, the exact drugs behind the clinical trial evidence in our research library. You're paying a premium for format and compounding, not for stronger evidence.

The FDA warning letter is worth weighing on its own terms

The FDA's September 2025 letter to Dermacare LLC, BlueChew's parent, was about marketing language, not a documented drug-interaction danger. It's a real, dated regulatory record, but a different and less severe category of concern than some other compounded ED products carry. Read it as one input among several, not the whole picture.

If you specifically want a chew, know what you're trading

Hims doesn't currently offer a chewable format for ED medication. If that format genuinely matters to you, BlueChew is one of the few options, but you're trading FDA-approved status, a lower per-dose price, and a cleaner regulatory record for it.

Frequently asked questions

Is BlueChew better than Hims?

On CitedRx's methodology, no: BlueChew scores 4.7/10 against Hims's 9.1/10. Hims prescribes plain FDA-approved sildenafil or tadalafil, the exact drugs tested in PDE5 inhibitor clinical trials, at roughly $2/dose. BlueChew's chews are compounded rather than FDA-approved, cost more per dose, and its parent company received an FDA warning letter in September 2025 over how it marketed them.

Is BlueChew cheaper than Hims?

Generally no. Hims's plain generic sildenafil or tadalafil typically runs close to $2 per dose. BlueChew's chews run roughly $2.65 to $3.33 per chew depending on the plan and source, meaningfully more per dose despite being the compounded, non-FDA-approved option.

Does Hims offer a chewable option like BlueChew?

Hims's core ED offering is a standard tablet, FDA-approved generic or brand-name sildenafil or tadalafil. If a chewable, non-swallow format is a priority for you, that's a genuine point in BlueChew's favor, though it comes from a compounded, not FDA-approved, product.

Why did BlueChew get an FDA warning letter?

The FDA sent a warning letter to BlueChew's parent company, Dermacare LLC, dated September 9, 2025, over marketing that described its compounded chewable tablets as containing the same active ingredients as FDA-approved drugs like Viagra and Cialis, a comparison the FDA considered misleading given that compounded products aren't themselves FDA-approved.
