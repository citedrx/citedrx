Hims vs. GoodRx Care: CitedRx's Top Two ED Providers Compared

Hims (9.1/10) and GoodRx Care (8.2/10) are ranked 1st and 2nd of 9 providers, and both prescribe FDA-approved medication: neither is the "worse" choice on evidence. Here's exactly where the 0.9-point gap comes from, and where GoodRx Care actually wins.

The short answer: Hims edges out GoodRx Care (9.1 vs 8.2) mainly on medication breadth and pricing flexibility, not evidence quality: both prescribe FDA-approved sildenafil and tadalafil, so the trial evidence applies equally to what either platform gives you. Hims offers both brand-name and generic in one place; GoodRx Care is generic-only, but its $18/mo bundles the visit, medication, and delivery into one predictable number.

What this means in plain language

The gap is about choice and flexibility, not evidence quality

Both providers prescribe FDA-approved sildenafil or tadalafil, the exact products with decades of PDE5 inhibitor trial history behind them, so the clinical evidence in our research library applies directly to what you'd get from either platform. Hims's edge comes mainly from offering brand-name medication alongside generic in one place, plus a flexible cancel-anytime subscription, not from a meaningful difference in what's actually been clinically tested.

GoodRx Care's price is simpler to predict

Hims's roughly $2/dose sounds cheap, but your real monthly cost depends entirely on how often you use it: a few doses a month is inexpensive, frequent use adds up. GoodRx Care's $18/mo is the same regardless of usage and already includes the visit and delivery, which makes it easier to budget even if it isn't always the lower number.

Hims's GLP-1 scrutiny is a different product line

Hims faces FDA scrutiny over its compounded weight-loss marketing, which is unrelated to its ED products: its sildenafil and tadalafil are the same FDA-approved generics GoodRx Care prescribes. Worth knowing about the company, but not a reason to discount its ED offering specifically.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hims better than GoodRx Care?

By CitedRx's scoring, yes: Hims scores 9.1/10 versus GoodRx Care's 8.2/10. The gap isn't about evidence quality, both prescribe FDA-approved sildenafil/tadalafil, so the trial evidence applies equally to either. It mainly reflects Hims offering brand-name and generic in one place, versus GoodRx Care's generic-only, simpler all-inclusive pricing.

Do Hims and GoodRx Care prescribe the same medications?

Mostly. Both prescribe FDA-approved generic sildenafil and tadalafil. Hims also offers brand-name Viagra and Cialis for patients who want it; GoodRx Care's ED offering is generic only.

Which is cheaper, Hims or GoodRx Care?

It depends on your usage. Hims bills roughly $2 per dose, so your monthly cost scales with how often you use it. GoodRx Care charges a flat $18/mo that bundles the visit, medication, and delivery. If you'd use ED medication more than about 9 times a month, GoodRx Care's flat rate likely comes out cheaper; less often, Hims's per-dose pricing likely wins.

Is Hims's GLP-1 scrutiny relevant to its ED products?

No. Hims faces FDA scrutiny over its compounded weight-loss (GLP-1) marketing, a separate product line entirely. Its ED products are FDA-approved sildenafil and tadalafil, unaffected by that issue, and CitedRx's regulatory-standing factor for Hims reflects only what's relevant to the ED product itself.
