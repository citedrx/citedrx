Hims vs. Rugiet: Is Rugiet's Compounded 3-in-1 Worth the Premium?

Rugiet's compounded sublingual troche costs roughly 7x what Hims typically runs for a comparable month of use, and scores 5.4/10 against Hims's 9.1/10. Here's what that price premium actually buys, evaluated factor by factor.

Last reviewed: October 2026 · See all 9 providers →
The short answer: Hims scores far higher than Rugiet (9.1 vs 5.4) and typically costs far less, since Hims's roughly $2/dose generic sildenafil is a fraction of Rugiet's $149/mo for most usage levels. Rugiet's entire lineup is compounded, meaning none of it is the plain FDA-approved sildenafil or tadalafil tested in PDE5 inhibitor clinical trials, and a recurring theme in its customer reviews is difficulty canceling the subscription.

What this means in plain language

You're paying more for a product with less direct evidence behind it

Rugiet's troche is custom-compounded, combining multiple medications into one dose, which isn't individually FDA-reviewed or the exact formulation tested in the pivotal sildenafil and tadalafil trials covered in our research library. Hims's plain generic sildenafil or tadalafil is the directly trial-tested product, at a fraction of the cost for typical usage.

"3-in-1" isn't automatically better than a single tested drug

Combining multiple compounds into one product sounds like more value, but it also means you can't isolate which ingredient is doing what, or rely on the dose-response data from trials that tested sildenafil and tadalafil individually. A single FDA-approved drug at a known, tested dose is the more evidence-grounded starting point for most people.

Check cancellation terms before you commit to either

Hims markets a cancel-anytime subscription; Rugiet's customer reviews repeatedly cite difficulty canceling, a real factor in CitedRx's pricing-and-support score. Read the current cancellation policy directly on either site before enrolling, not just the price.

Frequently asked questions

Is Rugiet worth the price compared to Hims?

On CitedRx's methodology, no: Rugiet costs roughly 7x more than Hims's typical usage cost ($149/mo vs ~$2/dose) while scoring far lower (5.4/10 vs 9.1/10). You're paying a premium for a compounded combination product that isn't the one tested in PDE5 inhibitor clinical trials, plus documented cancellation complaints.

What is Rugiet's 3-in-1 troche?

A compounded sublingual (under-the-tongue) troche combining multiple ED medications into one dose, typically sildenafil or tadalafil plus additional compounds. It's custom-mixed by a compounding pharmacy, not individually FDA-approved or tested in the pivotal PDE5 inhibitor trials the way plain sildenafil or tadalafil has been.

Does Rugiet offer a plain FDA-approved option?

No. Rugiet's entire lineup is compounded: there's no plain FDA-approved sildenafil or tadalafil option on its menu, unlike most other providers CitedRx compares in this category.

Is it hard to cancel a Rugiet subscription?

A recurring theme in Rugiet's customer reviews is difficulty canceling the subscription, which is reflected in its CitedRx pricing-and-support score. Check current cancellation terms directly before enrolling.
