QueerMed Review: Cost, Legitimacy, and What They Actually Prescribe

QueerMed is CitedRx's top-ranked gender-affirming HRT telehealth provider, a nationwide trans-specialty practice founded in 2017 by a named, credentialed physician. Here's what the evidence covers, what it costs, and where it's genuinely not the right fit.

Last reviewed: October 2026 · CitedRx Score: 8.6/10 · See all 6 providers →
The short answer: QueerMed scores 8.6/10 on CitedRx's methodology, ranked 1st of 6 gender-affirming HRT telehealth providers. It's a trans-specialty practice founded in 2017 by Dr. Izzy Lowell, serving adults nationwide with an informed-consent model aligned to WPATH SOC8 guidance. Pricing is sliding-scale self-pay rather than a published flat rate, with financial aid available through the Point of Pride HRT Access Fund. What the evidence actually covers

QueerMed uses an informed-consent model, which WPATH's Standards of Care Version 8 (2022) explicitly recognizes as a legitimate path to gender-affirming hormone therapy, not a shortcut around the older mental-health-referral-letter approach. See our evidence deep-dive for the full WPATH SOC8 and Endocrine Society guideline context this is measured against.

As a trans-specialty practice with a full team of physicians and nurse practitioners focused specifically on this care, rather than a general telehealth platform offering it as one of many services, QueerMed's clinical model is built around the lab-monitoring and timing nuance that specialization tends to handle better.

The genuine tradeoffs

  • Sliding-scale pricing plus a dedicated financial-aid fund for patients who qualify

Frequently asked questions

Is QueerMed legit?

Yes. QueerMed is a trans-specialty telehealth practice founded in 2017 by Dr. Izzy Lowell, with a full team of physicians and nurse practitioners focused specifically on gender-affirming care, serving adults nationwide. It scores 8.6/10 on CitedRx's own evidence-based methodology, currently ranked 1st of 6 gender-affirming HRT providers we compare.

How much does QueerMed cost?

QueerMed uses a sliding-scale self-pay model rather than a single published price, so your actual cost depends on your financial situation as assessed at enrollment. QueerMed also partners with the Point of Pride HRT Access Fund, which provides direct financial assistance to patients who can't otherwise afford hormone therapy. Confirm current pricing and aid eligibility directly with QueerMed.

Does QueerMed accept insurance?

QueerMed operates primarily as self-pay with sliding-scale pricing rather than billing major insurance directly, though you may still be able to use insurance for labs and prescriptions even if the visit itself isn't billed to insurance. If broad direct insurance billing matters most to you, FOLX Health is the stronger option in this comparison.

Is QueerMed available in my state?

QueerMed serves patients nationwide, a meaningful advantage over several trans-specialty competitors with state-limited access (QueerDoc, for example, is limited to 10 states). A clinician must still be licensed in the state where you're physically located at the time of the visit; confirm availability directly.

QueerMed vs FOLX Health: what's the difference?

QueerMed scores higher overall (8.6 vs 7.9), driven by its nationwide reach and a named, credentialed founder with a trans-specialty practice dating to 2017. FOLX Health's advantage is broader major-insurance acceptance (Aetna, BCBS, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare), versus QueerMed's sliding-scale self-pay model.
