QueerMed vs. QueerDoc: Is QueerDoc a Good Alternative?

QueerMed (8.6/10) and QueerDoc (7.7/10) are both trans-specialty telehealth practices using an informed-consent model and sliding-scale pricing. The real difference is reach: QueerMed serves patients nationwide, QueerDoc roughly 10 states. Here's the full breakdown.

The short answer: QueerMed scores higher than QueerDoc (8.6 vs 7.7), mainly because it's available nationwide while QueerDoc is licensed in roughly 10 states. Both are real, fully telehealth trans-specialty practices offering hormone therapy on a sliding scale with an informed-consent model, so QueerDoc is a legitimate option if you live in a state it serves, not a lesser care model. What this means in plain language

The score gap is about reach, not a lesser care model

Both practices are genuinely trans-specialized, using informed-consent prescribing covered in our evidence deep-dive. QueerDoc's lower score mainly reflects its narrower state licensure (roughly 10 states) and less independently verifiable rating data than QueerMed's longer, more documented track record, not a difference in how either delivers care.

QueerDoc covers more than hormone therapy

QueerDoc's services extend to puberty blockers, legal transition consultation (for gender marker changes), and surgery referrals, a broader scope than many GAHT-focused telehealth platforms. If you need that wider range of support in a state it serves, that's a genuine point in its favor beyond the raw score.

Frequently asked questions

Is QueerMed better than QueerDoc?

By CitedRx's scoring, yes: QueerMed scores 8.6/10 versus QueerDoc's 7.7/10. The main driver is reach: QueerMed serves patients nationwide, while QueerDoc is licensed in roughly 10 states. Both are trans-specialty practices using an informed-consent model, so the gap is about access, not a difference in how care is delivered.

What states does QueerDoc serve?

QueerDoc is licensed in approximately 10 states, far fewer than QueerMed's nationwide coverage. Confirm current state availability directly on QueerDoc's site before booking, since licensure can change.

Is QueerDoc a legitimate alternative to QueerMed?

Yes, for patients in a state QueerDoc serves. It's a real, fully telehealth trans-specialty practice offering hormone therapy, puberty blockers, legal transition consults, and surgery referrals on a sliding scale. It scores lower than QueerMed mainly on reach and independent-rating data, not because the care model itself is lesser.

Which is cheaper, QueerMed or QueerDoc?

Both use sliding-scale self-pay without a single fixed price. QueerDoc's own pricing page lists an adult initial visit around $300–500 on its current sliding scale; QueerMed doesn't publish a comparable fixed range and also offers financial aid through the Point of Pride HRT Access Fund. Confirm current figures directly with either provider, since sliding-scale pricing changes.
