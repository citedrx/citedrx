What every CitedRx research page covers

  1. Independent rating. Where available, we start from a recognized independent rating rather than a provider's self-reported reviews.
  2. Published pricing. We use the provider's own listed pricing, or figures independently reported by third-party comparison sites, and note when a price is a starting or promotional rate.
  3. Insurance & support model. We note what kind of support a program offers — insurance/prior-authorization help, coaching, monitoring — since this varies widely between providers.
  4. Update cadence & sources. Every research page lists its sources and the date it was last reviewed, so you can judge how current it is.
