How we source it

  1. Primary sources first. We start from the published trial or the recognized independent rating for a category, not a provider's self-reported claims.
  2. Every figure checked before publishing. Numbers are verified against the primary source (the paper, the FDA label, the original dataset) before they go on the page.
  3. Dated and re-reviewed. Every research page shows when it was last reviewed, and we revisit it as new evidence or pricing changes warrant.
