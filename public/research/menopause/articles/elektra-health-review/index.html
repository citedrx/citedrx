Elektra Health Review: Cost, Legitimacy, and What They Actually Prescribe

Elektra Health ties for CitedRx's top-ranked menopause/HRT telehealth provider, evidence-first by design and explicit about avoiding compounded bioidentical pellets. Here's what the evidence covers, what it costs, including a new 2026 no-cost insurance option, and where it's genuinely not the right fit.

What the trial evidence actually covers

Elektra Health's prescribing model defaults to FDA-approved hormone therapy and explicitly avoids compounded bioidentical pellets, a product category not reviewed by the FDA for safety or dosing consistency. That means the reinterpreted WHI evidence and current NAMS/ACOG guidance covered in our research library applies more directly to what Elektra Health patients are actually prescribed than it would at a provider offering compounded hormones as a standard option.

The platform's clinician network includes MDs, OB-GYNs, NPs, and CNMs, and 1:1 coaching is built into the program rather than offered as a separate add-on.

The genuine tradeoffs

What's genuinely good

  • Evidence-first prescribing that explicitly avoids compounded bioidentical pellets
  • 1:1 coaching included as a core part of the program
  • New 2026 $0 HRT option for eligible patients in 11 states via the HelloMeno ACA plan
  • Clinician network includes OB-GYNs and CNMs alongside MDs and NPs
  • Reports being in-network with many New York insurance plans specifically

The real tradeoffs

  • $249 cash-pay consult if you're not eligible for HelloMeno or another accepted plan
  • HelloMeno's no-cost HRT is limited to 11 states and a specific ACA marketplace plan, not available nationally
  • No independent third-party rating specific to Elektra comparable to what's available for other CitedRx categories
  • Insurance network is still described as "expanding" outside New York and the HelloMeno states, so broad national coverage isn't guaranteed yet

Frequently asked questions

Is Elektra Health legit?

Yes. Elektra Health is a menopause-specialty telehealth platform staffed by MDs, OB-GYNs, NPs, and CNMs, prescribing FDA-approved hormone therapy first and explicitly avoiding compounded bioidentical pellets. It scores 8.5/10 on CitedRx's own evidence-based methodology, tied for 1st of 9 menopause providers we compare.

How much does Elektra Health cost?

An initial cash-pay consult runs $249. As of 2026, eligible patients in 11 states can get HRT at no cost through Elektra's Oscar Health "HelloMeno" ACA marketplace plan; outside that plan, Elektra's payer network is expanding (nearly 40 partners), so confirm your specific coverage directly.

Does Elektra Health accept insurance?

It's expanding: nearly 40 payer partners as of this review, and Elektra says it's in-network with many New York plans, covering an estimated 95%+ of women in New York specifically. Nationally, the clearest no-cost path is the 2026 HelloMeno ACA plan in Arizona, Florida, Georgia, Iowa, Missouri, Nebraska, North Carolina, Ohio, Oklahoma, Tennessee, and Texas.

What is Elektra Health's HelloMeno plan?

HelloMeno is a 2026 ACA marketplace health plan Elektra Health launched with Oscar Health, available in 11 states. It includes no-cost hormone replacement therapy, primary care, gynecology, behavioral health visits, and labs for enrolled members, with Elektra and Oscar estimating members may save up to roughly $900 a year.

Elektra Health vs Gennev: what's the difference?

Elektra Health emphasizes explicitly avoiding compounded bioidentical pellets plus included 1:1 coaching; Gennev's network is exclusively board-certified OB-GYNs with major insurance (Aetna, Anthem, UnitedHealthcare) already in place.
