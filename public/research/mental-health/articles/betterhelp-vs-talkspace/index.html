BetterHelp vs. Talkspace: The Two Biggest Names, Compared Honestly

BetterHelp and Talkspace are the two most recognized online therapy brands, with a narrow 0.2-point score gap (7.1 vs 7.3) that comes down to insurance acceptance and a real regulatory note: BetterHelp's 2023 FTC settlement over sharing sensitive mental health data.

What this means in plain language

The FTC settlement is the most important thing to know about BetterHelp

In 2023, the FTC finalized an order against BetterHelp over sharing users' mental health questionnaire answers and other sensitive data with advertisers like Facebook and Snapchat, despite promising not to, resulting in a $7.8 million payment and restrictions on future data practices. This is a civil settlement, not a finding that current BetterHelp care is unsafe, but it's directly relevant in a category where data sensitivity matters more than almost anywhere else CitedRx covers, which is why it's reflected in BetterHelp's regulatory-standing score.

Talkspace's insurance acceptance is the practical advantage

BetterHelp doesn't bill insurance directly at all, meaning every patient pays the self-pay rate (offset partly by financial aid for those who qualify). Talkspace's broader insurance acceptance can make it meaningfully cheaper than its sticker price for patients with eligible coverage.

Neither replaces crisis or specialized psychiatric care

Both are scoped to standard talk therapy and are not crisis services: if you're in a mental health crisis, call or text 988, available 24/7. Neither platform prescribes medication the way Brightside or Talkiatry do; if you need SSRI/SNRI management specifically, check our full comparison for providers that include it.

Frequently asked questions

Is BetterHelp or Talkspace better?

By a narrow margin, Talkspace scores higher on CitedRx's methodology (7.3/10 vs 7.1/10), driven mainly by broader direct insurance acceptance and a regulatory-standing deduction on BetterHelp's score tied to its 2023 FTC settlement. Both offer large-scale, broadly accessible talk therapy.

What happened with BetterHelp and the FTC?

In 2023, the FTC finalized an order against BetterHelp requiring a $7.8 million payment to settle allegations that it shared users' sensitive health information, including mental health questionnaire answers, with advertisers like Facebook and Snapchat without adequate consent, despite promising not to share such data. The order also restricts BetterHelp's future data-sharing practices.

Does BetterHelp accept insurance?

No, BetterHelp does not bill insurance directly, which offsets its scale advantage for cost-sensitive users; it does offer financial aid for qualifying patients. Talkspace accepts broader insurance, which can make it cheaper than BetterHelp's flat rate for in-network patients.

Which is cheaper, BetterHelp or Talkspace?

BetterHelp's self-pay rate (~$260/mo) is slightly lower than Talkspace's (~$276/mo) as a flat sticker price. But Talkspace's broader insurance acceptance can make it cheaper in practice for insured patients, since BetterHelp requires self-pay regardless of your coverage.
