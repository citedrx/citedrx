Hone Health Review: Cost, Legitimacy, and Lab Process

What the evidence covers

Hone Health's prescribing is gated behind lab-confirmed low testosterone, the diagnostic threshold studied in the 2023 TRAVERSE trial and the NIH-funded T Trials covered in our research library. Its at-home panel tests more than 40 biomarkers, not just total testosterone, which gives a fuller picture than a minimal single-marker test, and repeat testing every 3-6 months tracks whether levels and related markers stay in range once treatment starts.

This lab-first structure is the clearest quality signal CitedRx weighs most heavily in this category: a provider testing and monitoring testosterone directly, rather than prescribing off a symptom questionnaire alone, is treating the diagnosed condition the trial evidence actually applies to.

The genuine tradeoffs

What's genuinely strong

  • At-home 40+ biomarker panel required before prescribing, with recurring retesting
  • 4.8/5 Trustpilot rating across 11,150+ reviews, the largest independent review base among TRT providers CitedRx compares
  • Tied for the highest CitedRx Score in this category (9.4/10)

The real tradeoffs

  • Priced higher ($135-155/mo) than some other lab-first, similarly-scored providers
  • At-home finger-stick testing is convenient but generally less precise than an in-person venous draw
  • Pricing isn't published as a single flat number, so your exact cost depends on what's prescribed

Frequently asked questions

Is Hone Health legit?

Yes. Hone Health requires an at-home, 40+ biomarker finger-stick lab panel before prescribing, matching the lab-confirmed diagnostic standard the TRAVERSE and T Trials evidence applies to. It holds a 4.8/5 Trustpilot rating across more than 11,150 reviews, the highest review volume of any TRT provider CitedRx compares, and scores 9.4/10, tied for the top CitedRx Score in this category.

How much does Hone Health cost?

Hone Health's membership plus medication runs approximately $135-155 per month, with labs repeated every 3-6 months. Confirm current pricing directly, since plan structures can change.

Does Hone Health require lab testing?

Yes, and it's a core part of its model: an at-home finger-stick panel covering 40+ biomarkers is required before prescribing, not just a symptom questionnaire, with repeat testing every 3-6 months to monitor levels.

Hone Health vs Peter MD: what's the difference?

Both tie at 9.4/10 and both require labs, but Peter MD's flat $79-99/mo is meaningfully cheaper than Hone Health's $135-155/mo. The difference is largely in testing format and to a lesser extent brand positioning: Hone Health uses at-home finger-stick testing with the largest review base in the category; Peter MD bundles a baseline panel into a lower flat fee.
