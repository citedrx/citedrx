Hone Health vs. Peter MD: CitedRx's Tied Top TRT Providers Compared

Hone Health and Peter MD both score 9.4/10, CitedRx's highest in this category, yet Peter MD costs $56-76 less per month. Here's exactly what that price gap buys, and doesn't.

Last reviewed: November 2026 · See all 9 providers →
The short answer: Hone Health and Peter MD are tied at 9.4/10, CitedRx's top score among the 9 TRT providers we compare, and both require lab-confirmed diagnosis before prescribing. The real difference is price and testing format: Peter MD's flat $79-99/mo with a bundled baseline panel is meaningfully cheaper than Hone Health's $135-155/mo, while Hone Health's at-home 40+ biomarker panel and 11,150+ Trustpilot reviews are the broadest testing and review base in the category.

What this means in plain language

A tied score means neither is the objectively "better" choice

Both providers test and monitor lab-confirmed testosterone levels, the diagnostic standard the TRAVERSE and T Trials evidence actually applies to, and both hold strong independent ratings. A tied 9.4/10 means this comes down to which tradeoffs fit you, not a quality gap.

Peter MD's flat pricing is the more budget-predictable option

Peter MD bundles a baseline lab panel and follow-up testing into one flat $79-99/mo fee, a meaningfully lower and more predictable cost than Hone Health's $135-155/mo range. If cost predictability matters most, Peter MD is the stronger pick at an equal score.

Hone Health's broader panel and review base are real, not just marketing

Hone Health's 40+ biomarker at-home panel covers more ground than Peter MD's baseline tests, and its 11,150+ Trustpilot reviews are the most heavily documented independent track record of any TRT provider CitedRx compares. If broader lab visibility and the deepest review history matter more to you than price, that's a genuine reason to choose Hone Health despite the higher cost.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hone Health better than Peter MD?

Neither: they tie at 9.4/10, CitedRx's top score in this category. Both require lab-confirmed diagnosis before prescribing. The practical difference is price and testing format, not evidence match or quality of care.

Which is cheaper, Hone Health or Peter MD?

Peter MD, by a meaningful margin: its flat $79-99/mo includes a baseline lab panel, versus Hone Health's $135-155/mo membership plus medication. Both require labs; the price gap reflects testing format and brand positioning more than evidence quality.

What's the difference in lab testing between Hone Health and Peter MD?

Hone Health uses an at-home, 40+ biomarker finger-stick panel repeated every 3-6 months. Peter MD requires a baseline panel (free/total T, LH, FSH, PSA, CBC) with follow-up labs bundled into its flat monthly fee. Hone Health's panel is broader; Peter MD's is more focused and cheaper.

Which has more independent reviews, Hone Health or Peter MD?

Hone Health has the larger confirmed review count (4.8/5 across 11,150+ Trustpilot reviews). Peter MD is reported to have the highest review volume among the lab-first, top-rated providers in some tracking, with a comparable 4.7-5/5 rating, though the exact count is less precisely documented than Hone Health's.
