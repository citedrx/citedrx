Maximus Tribe vs. TRT Nation: Nearly Identical Price, Very Different Score

$99.99/mo vs $99/mo, but 8.3/10 vs 7.3/10. Here's what the nearly identical sticker price doesn't tell you about these two TRT providers.

Last reviewed: November 2026 · See all 9 providers →
The short answer: Maximus Tribe and TRT Nation cost almost exactly the same ($99.99/mo vs $99/mo), but Maximus Tribe scores a full point higher (8.3 vs 7.3). The difference isn't evidence match: both require lab-confirmed diagnosis. It's independent rating: TRT Nation's Trustpilot score (2.0/5, 28 reviews) is dramatically below every other provider in this category, most of which sit at 4.7-4.8/5, while Maximus Tribe also offers a broader range of medication formats.

What this means in plain language

Both treat the diagnosed condition the trial evidence applies to

Maximus Tribe and TRT Nation both require lab-confirmed low testosterone before prescribing, the diagnostic standard studied in the TRAVERSE and T Trials. Evidence match isn't what separates these two: the gap is almost entirely about patient experience and format flexibility.

TRT Nation's rating is the real story behind the score gap

A 2.0/5 rating from 28 Trustpilot reviews stands out sharply against a category where most providers sit at 4.7-4.8/5. That's a specific, consistent signal, not statistical noise from a tiny sample, and it's the single biggest factor pulling TRT Nation's score down to 7.3/10 despite its competitive flat price.

Maximus Tribe's format flexibility is a genuine differentiator

Offering oral, cream, and injectable testosterone options at essentially the same price as TRT Nation's single format gives patients more say in how they take their medication, a real practical advantage independent of the rating gap.

Frequently asked questions

Is Maximus Tribe better than TRT Nation?

By CitedRx's scoring, yes: Maximus Tribe scores 8.3/10 against TRT Nation's 7.3/10, despite nearly identical pricing ($99.99/mo vs $99/mo). The gap comes mainly from TRT Nation's notably weak independent rating (2.0/5 Trustpilot, 28 reviews) versus Maximus Tribe's broader medication options.

Why does TRT Nation score lower if it's the same price as Maximus Tribe?

Price is only one factor in CitedRx's methodology. TRT Nation's Trustpilot rating (2.0/5, 28 reviews) is dramatically lower than every other TRT provider CitedRx compares, most of which sit at 4.7-4.8/5, and that independent-rating gap is the main driver of its lower score at an almost identical price to Maximus Tribe.

What medication formats does Maximus Tribe offer?

Maximus Tribe offers oral, cream, and injectable testosterone options, a broader range of delivery formats than TRT Nation's offering, giving patients more flexibility in how they take their medication.

Is TRT Nation legitimate despite the low rating?

TRT Nation does require lab-confirmed diagnosis before prescribing, like every provider CitedRx compares in this category, so it isn't operating outside the evidence base. But a 2.0/5 rating from 28 reviews is a real, consistent signal of below-average patient experience, not noise from a tiny sample, and it's a legitimate reason for caution.
