Found Review: Cost, Legitimacy, and What They Actually Prescribe

Found is a licensed GLP-1 telehealth provider offering both FDA-approved branded medication and, for some patients, compounded semaglutide, with coaching included in every plan. Here's what the evidence covers, what it costs, and where it genuinely falls short of the branded-only competition.

Last reviewed: September 2026 · CitedRx Score: 8.3/10 · See all 15 providers →
The short answer: Found scores 8.3/10 on CitedRx's methodology, ranked 4th of 15 GLP-1 telehealth providers, with a 4.6/5 U.S. News rating. It prescribes both FDA-approved branded GLP-1s (including Zepbound) and compounded semaglutide depending on your plan and eligibility, paired with included coaching and a dedicated prior-authorization team for insured members. What the trial evidence actually covers

Found's branded tier dispenses the same FDA-approved medications studied in the pivotal GLP-1 trials: semaglutide (Wegovy) and tirzepatide (Zepbound), at the doses tested in STEP and SURMOUNT. That evidence, including STEP 1's 14.9% average weight loss and SURMOUNT-1's 20.9%, applies directly to what you'd be prescribed on Found's branded tier.

Found's compounded tier is a different product: compounded semaglutide made by a 503A/503B pharmacy rather than the original manufacturer. As we explain in detail on our full evidence page, no published trial has tested the compounded version specifically, so that trial data doesn't transfer automatically. This isn't unique to Found: it's the same distinction we apply to every provider offering a compounded option.

Frequently asked questions

Is Found legit?

Yes. Found is a licensed telehealth platform prescribing both FDA-approved branded GLP-1s (including Zepbound) and, for some patients, compounded semaglutide through 503A/503B pharmacies. It holds a 4.6/5 rating on U.S. News's telehealth comparison and scores 8.3/10 on CitedRx's own evidence-based methodology, currently ranked 4th of 15 providers we compare.

How much does Found cost?

Found's membership runs approximately $49 to $99 per month, varying by whether you're prescribed the branded or compounded tier and by insurance status. Confirm your specific price directly with Found before enrolling, since it depends on which medication tier you're actually eligible for.

Does Found accept insurance?

Found includes a dedicated prior-authorization team for commercially insured members seeking coverage for branded medication, which is a meaningful support feature for navigating insurance approval. Confirm your specific plan's coverage directly, since GLP-1 coverage varies widely by insurer.

What does Found prescribe?

Both FDA-approved branded GLP-1s (including Zepbound) and compounded semaglutide, dispensed through 503A/503B compounding pharmacies, depending on your plan and eligibility. This is CitedRx's "Mixed" tier: the trial evidence on our research page applies directly to the branded tier, not the compounded one, so ask which one you'd actually be prescribed before enrolling.

Found vs Ro: what's the difference?

Ro prescribes branded GLP-1s exclusively, which is why it scores higher (9.3 vs 8.3) on CitedRx's evidence-match factor specifically. Found offers both branded and compounded options with included coaching at a lower starting price. See our full Found vs. Ro comparison for a factor-by-factor breakdown.
