Found vs. Noom Med: Coaching-Led GLP-1 Providers Compared

Found (8.3/10) and Noom Med (7.2/10) both pair GLP-1 prescribing with behavior-change coaching, but the models and the price gap are substantial. Here's the factor-by-factor breakdown.

The short answer: Found scores higher than Noom Med (8.3 vs 7.2), holds a slightly higher independent rating (4.6/5 vs 4.3/5), includes a dedicated prior-authorization team where Noom Med's insurance support is more limited, and starts at roughly a third of Noom Med's $149/mo. What this means in plain language

Both mix medication tiers, so ask which one you'd actually get

Neither Found nor Noom Med is exclusively branded. Both offer compounded semaglutide to some patients and brand-name GLP-1s (including Zepbound, on Found's side) to others, depending on eligibility and plan. Only the branded tier is the one directly covered by the STEP and SURMOUNT trial data, on either platform, so this is the single most important question to ask before enrolling with either.

Found's insurance support is the more concrete offering

Found includes a dedicated prior-authorization team for commercially insured members, a real, specific service. Noom Med's insurance support is comparatively limited, which matters most if you're hoping to get a branded GLP-1 covered rather than paying cash.

Noom Med's app-based coaching is a genuinely different product, not just a feature

Noom Med isn't simply "coaching bundled with a prescription": it layers GLP-1 prescribing onto Noom's existing psychology-based behavior-change app and its structured daily habit-tracking model, built over more than a decade of consumer weight-loss coaching. If that specific app-driven format is what you're looking for, it's a real differentiator Found doesn't offer, even though Found scores higher and costs less on CitedRx's methodology.

Frequently asked questions

Is Found better than Noom Med?

On CitedRx's methodology, yes: Found scores 8.3/10 against Noom Med's 7.2/10, holds a higher independent rating (4.6/5 vs 4.3/5), and includes a dedicated prior-authorization team where Noom Med's insurance support is more limited. Found's membership also starts well below Noom Med's $149/mo.

What's the real difference between Found and Noom Med's coaching?

Both pair GLP-1 prescribing with coaching, but the models differ: Found includes coaching as part of its standard membership alongside medical support, while Noom Med layers GLP-1 prescribing onto Noom's existing psychology-based behavior-change app, built around structured daily habit tracking. Which model fits depends on whether you want an app-driven behavior program or a more traditional membership structure.

Is Found or Noom Med cheaper?

Found. Its membership runs $49 to $99 per month depending on your prescribed tier, versus Noom Med's $149 per month. Medication is typically billed separately on both platforms, and actual total cost depends heavily on insurance coverage and which medication you're prescribed.

Do Found and Noom Med both offer brand-name GLP-1s?

Both offer branded options for some patients, but neither is exclusively branded: Found offers compounded semaglutide and brand-name options including Zepbound depending on eligibility, and Noom Med prescribes compounded or brand-name medication depending on your plan. On both platforms, only the branded tier is the one directly covered by the GLP-1 clinical trial evidence; ask which tier you'd actually be prescribed.
