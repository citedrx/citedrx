Found vs. Ro: Which GLP-1 Provider Fits You?

Ro (9.3/10) and Found (8.3/10) are ranked 1st and 4th of 15 providers on CitedRx's methodology, and the gap comes down to one clear factor: Ro prescribes branded medication almost exclusively, while Found mixes in a compounded option. Here's the honest, factor-by-factor breakdown, including where Found actually comes out ahead.

Last reviewed: September 2026 · See all 15 providers →
The short answer: Ro scores higher overall (9.3 vs 8.3) specifically because it prescribes branded GLP-1s almost exclusively, which is what CitedRx's Evidence Match factor rewards. But Found holds a slightly higher independent rating (4.6/5 vs 4.4/5), includes coaching in its membership where Ro does not, and starts at a lower monthly price. Neither is a bad choice: which one fits depends on whether guaranteed branded-only prescribing is your top priority, or whether cost and included coaching matter more.

What this means in plain language

The evidence-match gap is real, and it's the whole reason for the score difference

Ro's menu is predominantly FDA-approved branded product (Wegovy, Zepbound, Ozempic), so the STEP and SURMOUNT trial data applies directly to what most Ro patients are actually prescribed. Found offers that same branded product to some patients and compounded semaglutide to others, which is exactly the distinction CitedRx's scoring is designed to surface, not a knock on Found's legitimacy.

On rating and coaching, Found edges ahead

Found's 4.6/5 U.S. News rating is slightly higher than Ro's 4.4/5, and Found includes coaching as part of its standard membership, which Ro doesn't highlight as included. If ongoing behavior-change support matters to you as much as the medication itself, that's a genuine point in Found's favor.

Price isn't a clean comparison until you know your tier

Found's $49–$99/mo range starts lower than Ro's $99+/mo compounded cash-pay tier, but both providers' actual cost depends heavily on insurance coverage and which specific medication you're prescribed. Treat these as starting points to verify directly, not a final answer.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ro better than Found?

On CitedRx's evidence-match factor specifically, yes: Ro prescribes branded GLP-1s exclusively, which is why it scores 9.3/10 versus Found's 8.3/10 overall. But Found actually holds a slightly higher independent rating (4.6/5 vs Ro's 4.4/5), includes coaching in its membership, and starts at a lower price. "Better" depends on whether guaranteed branded-only prescribing matters more to you than cost and included coaching.

Is Found or Ro cheaper?

Found's membership starts lower, around $49 to $99 per month depending on your prescribed tier, versus Ro's compounded cash-pay tier starting around $99 per month. Actual cost for either depends heavily on insurance coverage and which medication you're prescribed, so confirm directly before comparing headline prices.

Does Ro or Found have better insurance support?

Both include a dedicated prior-authorization team for insured members seeking branded-medication coverage, a real, comparable feature on both platforms.

Which one prescribes only FDA-approved medication?

Neither is exclusively branded. Ro's menu is predominantly FDA-approved branded product with compounded options for cash-pay flexibility; Found offers both branded and compounded depending on your plan and eligibility. Ro's branded-primary model is why it scores higher on evidence match, but check which tier you'd actually be prescribed on either platform.
