Ro Review: Cost, Legitimacy, and What They Actually Prescribe

Ro is CitedRx's top-ranked GLP-1 telehealth provider, predominantly branded with a compounded cash-pay fallback and a dedicated prior-authorization team. Here's what the evidence covers, what it costs, and where it's genuinely not the right fit.

Last reviewed: October 2026 · CitedRx Score: 9.3/10 · See all 15 providers →
The short answer: Ro scores 9.3/10 on CitedRx's methodology, ranked 1st of 15 GLP-1 telehealth providers, with a 4.4/5 U.S. News rating. It prescribes predominantly FDA-approved branded GLP-1s (Wegovy, Zepbound, Ozempic), including the Wegovy oral pill, with a dedicated prior-authorization team for insured members, plus a compounded cash-pay tier from $99/mo for patients without insurance coverage. What the trial evidence actually covers

Ro's menu is predominantly FDA-approved branded product: semaglutide (Wegovy) and tirzepatide (Zepbound), at the doses tested in STEP and SURMOUNT. That evidence, including STEP 1's 14.9% average weight loss and SURMOUNT-1's 20.9%, applies directly to what most Ro patients are actually prescribed, which is the main reason it scores highest on CitedRx's Evidence Match factor among the 15 providers we compare.

Ro also offers a compounded semaglutide cash-pay tier as a lower-cost fallback. As we explain in detail on our full evidence page, no published trial has tested the compounded version specifically, so that trial data doesn't transfer automatically to that tier. Ask which tier you'd actually be prescribed before enrolling.

The genuine tradeoffs

What's genuinely good

  • Broadest branded medication selection of any provider we track, including both Wegovy and Zepbound
  • Offers the Wegovy oral pill, not just injectable, for patients who prefer it
  • Dedicated prior-authorization team for insured members
  • 4.4/5 U.S. News rating, among the highest-rated providers we compare
  • Compounded cash-pay fallback for patients without insurance coverage

The real tradeoffs

  • Branded pricing isn't published as a single headline number: your actual cost depends on insurance and which drug you're prescribed
  • No coaching or behavior-change program highlighted, unlike Found or Noom Med
  • The compounded cash-pay tier carries the same evidence caveat as any compounded provider: it's not the trial-tested product

Frequently asked questions

Is Ro legit?

Yes. Ro is one of the largest U.S. telehealth platforms, prescribing predominantly FDA-approved branded GLP-1s (Wegovy, Zepbound, Ozempic) with a dedicated prior-authorization team for insured members. It holds a 4.4/5 rating on U.S. News's telehealth comparison and scores 9.3/10 on CitedRx's own evidence-based methodology, currently ranked 1st of 15 providers we compare.

How much does Ro cost?

Ro's compounded cash-pay tier starts from $99/month. Branded medication pricing depends heavily on your insurance coverage and which drug you're prescribed; confirm your specific price directly with Ro before enrolling.

Does Ro accept insurance?

Ro includes a dedicated prior-authorization team for commercially insured members seeking coverage for branded medication. Confirm your specific plan's coverage directly, since GLP-1 coverage varies widely by insurer.

What does Ro prescribe?

Predominantly FDA-approved branded GLP-1s: Wegovy and Zepbound, available as a weekly injection or, for Wegovy, a daily oral pill, plus Ozempic. Ro also offers a compounded semaglutide cash-pay tier for patients who want a lower entry price or don't have insurance coverage.

Ro vs Found: what's the difference?

Ro prescribes predominantly branded GLP-1s, which is why it scores higher (9.3 vs 8.3) on CitedRx's evidence-match factor specifically. Found offers more of a mixed branded-and-compounded menu with included coaching at a lower starting price. See our full Found vs. Ro comparison for a factor-by-factor breakdown.
