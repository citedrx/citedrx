Ro vs. LifeMD: CitedRx's Top Two GLP-1 Providers Compared

Ro (9.3/10) and LifeMD (9.1/10) are ranked 1st and 2nd of 15 providers, and both are branded-primary: neither is the obvious "worse" choice. Here's exactly where the 0.2-point gap comes from, and where LifeMD actually has the edge.

Last reviewed: September 2026 · See all 15 providers →
The short answer: Ro edges out LifeMD (9.3 vs 9.1) mainly on independent rating (4.4/5 vs 4.0/5 on U.S. News), not on medication access: both prescribe FDA-approved branded GLP-1s, including the Wegovy pill, with a dedicated prior-authorization team for insured members. Ro also offers a compounded cash-pay tier for a published lower entry price, which LifeMD's branded-only focus doesn't include.

What this means in plain language

The gap is mostly about independent rating, not access

Both providers prescribe the exact FDA-approved branded product tested in the STEP and SURMOUNT trials, so the clinical-trial evidence in our research library applies directly to what you'd likely be prescribed on either platform. The 0.2-point score difference comes almost entirely from Ro's higher U.S. News rating (4.4/5 vs 4.0/5), not from a meaningful gap in what medication you'd actually get.

Ro gives you a lower-cost fallback; LifeMD doesn't

Ro publishes a starting price from its compounded cash-pay tier ($99/mo), which gives cash-pay patients a clear lower-cost option if branded pricing or insurance coverage doesn't work out. LifeMD's "best for" is specifically the brand-name path; it doesn't offer that same compounded fallback, which is also why its menu is simpler to evaluate if branded-only is what you want from the start.

Neither leads on coaching, unlike Found or Noom Med

If structured coaching or behavior-change support matters as much to you as the medication itself, neither Ro nor LifeMD highlights that as a core part of the membership the way Found or Noom Med do. Both are built primarily around medication access and insurance coordination, not a coaching program.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ro better than LifeMD?

By a narrow margin on CitedRx's methodology: Ro scores 9.3/10 versus LifeMD's 9.1/10, both branded-primary. The gap comes mostly from independent rating (Ro 4.4/5 vs LifeMD 4.0/5 on U.S. News) rather than a difference in medication access: both prescribe FDA-approved branded GLP-1s, including the Wegovy pill, with a dedicated prior-authorization team.

Do Ro and LifeMD prescribe the same medications?

Largely yes. Both prescribe Wegovy (semaglutide, injectable or the oral pill) and Zepbound (tirzepatide). Ro also offers a compounded cash-pay tier as a lower-cost option; LifeMD's menu is more narrowly focused on the brand-name path.

Which is cheaper, Ro or LifeMD?

Ro publishes a starting price (from $99/mo for its compounded cash-pay tier); LifeMD's pricing varies by plan and isn't published as a single headline number. Actual cost for either depends heavily on insurance coverage and which medication you're prescribed, so confirm directly before comparing.

Do both offer insurance support?

Yes. Both Ro and LifeMD include a dedicated prior-authorization team for insured members seeking coverage for branded medication, a comparable feature on both platforms.
