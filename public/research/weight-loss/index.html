Our research methodology: GLP-1 weight loss telehealth

This page explains exactly how CitedRx researches and ranks GLP-1 weight loss telehealth programs — where the ratings come from, how we verify pricing, and how often we revisit it.

Last reviewed: August 2026 · ← Research hub · See the current rankings →

1. Independent rating

Where an independent rating exists, we start there instead of a provider's self-reported testimonials. For GLP-1 telehealth, that's U.S. News & World Report's 2026–2027 Best GLP-1 Telehealth Platforms ratings, which score providers on factors like patient experience, medical oversight, and value. Programs outside that rated set (Henry Meds, Mochi Health, WeightWatchers Clinic, Calibrate) are included in a separate "also worth considering" section on the rankings page rather than given an invented star rating.

2. Published pricing, verified where possible

We use each provider's own listed pricing where available. Where a provider doesn't publish pricing clearly, we use figures independently reported by third-party comparison and review sites, cross-checked against more than one source where possible. GLP-1 pricing moves often — promotional first-month rates, compounding availability shifts, and plan-length discounts all affect the number a patient actually sees — so every price on our rankings page is labeled with what it covers (e.g. "membership before medication," "compounded semaglutide") rather than presented as one flat number.

3. Insurance & prior-authorization support

For patients with commercial insurance, how fast a program can secure prior authorization for a brand-name GLP-1 often matters more than the cash price. We note which programs have a dedicated prior-authorization team (Found, Ro, LifeMD, WeightWatchers Clinic) versus programs built primarily around cash-pay compounded medication with lighter insurance support.

4. Clinical support model

Two programs can prescribe the same medication and still offer very different care. We note whether coaching, lab monitoring, and specialist prescribers are included, since programs range from fast, low-friction intake (Henry Meds) to higher-touch clinical models with quarterly monitoring (Mochi Health, Calibrate).

How often we update this

GLP-1 pricing and provider lineups shift often enough that a "set and forget" ranking goes stale within months. We review this page and the rankings it feeds at least quarterly, or sooner if a major pricing change or a new independent rating is published. The "last reviewed" date at the top of this page and the rankings page reflects the most recent pass.

Corrections & disclosures

Ranking order reflects the factors above, not payment from a provider. CitedRx may earn a referral fee if a reader signs up with a provider through a link on this site; that revenue does not influence ranking order. If you spot pricing or availability that's out of date, treat the provider's own site as the source of truth for what you'll actually be charged before enrolling.
