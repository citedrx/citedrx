What this means in plain language

The cheapest stable-priced provider is also tied for the top score

Peter MD's flat $79-99/mo with labs bundled in is both the lowest ongoing price on this page and tied for CitedRx's highest score (9.4/10) in this category. Unlike some verticals CitedRx covers, there's no real tradeoff here between price and evidence match: all 9 TRT providers require lab-confirmed diagnosis before prescribing.

A flat low price doesn't guarantee a good experience

TRT Nation's $99/mo flat rate looks competitive against peers charging $135-225/mo, but its Trustpilot rating (2.0/5, 28 reviews) is dramatically lower than every other provider on this list, most of which sit at 4.7-4.8/5. A low price with a weak independent rating is a real warning sign, not just noise.

Annual pricing can hide a competitive monthly rate

Defy Medical's ~$2,400-$3,000/yr estimate sounds steep stated as a lump sum, but it works out to roughly $200-250/mo, in line with mid-tier monthly providers, for comprehensive, optimization-focused lab work. Don't let the billing period alone make a provider look more or less expensive than it actually is.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest TRT telehealth provider?

Low T Center advertises a $25 first-test offer, but that's a one-time intro price, not an ongoing monthly cost. Among providers with a stable published monthly price, Peter MD is cheapest at $79-99/mo with labs bundled in, and it's also tied for CitedRx's highest score (9.4/10) in this category.

Is the cheapest TRT provider the best one?

Not automatically, but in this category the cheapest stable-priced option, Peter MD, is also tied for the top CitedRx Score. The real caution is TRT Nation: a flat $99/mo sounds appealing, but its Trustpilot rating (2.0/5, 28 reviews) is well below every peer, which is reflected in its 7.3/10 score.

Why does Defy Medical show an annual price instead of monthly?

Defy Medical publishes its comprehensive, optimization-focused program as an all-in annual estimate (~$2,400-$3,000/yr) rather than a flat monthly fee, since its lab panel and visit cadence can vary by patient. We've placed it on this page by its approximate monthly equivalent (~$200-250/mo) for comparability, but confirm the current structure directly.
