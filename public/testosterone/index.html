How we scored these

  1. Evidence Match (4 pts). Full credit requires lab-confirmed hypogonadism before prescribing — the diagnostic standard the TRAVERSE and T Trials evidence actually applies to. All 9 providers here require labs.
  2. Independent Rating (2 pts). Scaled from the best available third-party rating (primarily Trustpilot), weighted conservatively where sources conflict.
  3. Pricing & Support (2 pts). Transparent flat or bundled pricing scores highest; pay-per-service models with separately billed labs score lower for clarity, even when overall quality is high.
  4. Lab Rigor (1 pt). Breadth of the panel and frequency of retesting — comprehensive, recurring panels score highest.
  5. Regulatory Standing (1 pt). Full credit by default; deducted only for issues tied specifically to the TRT service itself, not an unrelated product line.

Frequently asked questions

Is TRT safe for your heart?

The 2023 TRAVERSE trial (n=5,246, NEJM) found testosterone therapy was not associated with an increased rate of major adverse cardiovascular events versus placebo (7.0% vs 7.3%, noninferior). It did find higher rates of atrial fibrillation (3.5% vs 2.4%) and venous thromboembolism (1.7% vs 1.2%) as secondary findings, so cardiovascular monitoring still matters, but the core heart-attack/stroke/CV-death fear was not borne out.

What's the difference between hypogonadism and "Low T"?

Hypogonadism is a diagnosed clinical condition confirmed by lab-tested low testosterone (typically under 300 ng/dL on two morning blood draws) plus symptoms — this is the FDA-approved indication and what clinical trials like TRAVERSE studied. "Low T" is a marketing term, not a clinical diagnosis. A legitimate provider requires bloodwork before prescribing; one that doesn't is prescribing outside what the evidence actually supports.

Why does lab testing matter so much when choosing a TRT provider?

Because it's the clearest quality signal in this category: a provider that requires lab-confirmed low testosterone before prescribing is treating a diagnosed condition the trial evidence actually applies to, while a provider prescribing off a symptom questionnaire alone is not. Every provider CitedRx ranked in this category does require labs — but the rigor varies.

Does TRT help with cognition or energy?

Not according to the NIH-funded T Trials (2016): testosterone improved sexual function, mood, and bone density in older men with confirmed low T, but showed no meaningful improvement in energy/vitality or cognitive function.

Is testosterone a controlled substance?

Yes — testosterone is a Schedule III controlled substance. Legitimate telehealth providers require lab-confirmed diagnosis and appropriate prescribing safeguards, not just an online questionnaire.

How much does TRT telehealth cost per month?

Typically $99-$300/month including medication, though some providers bill labs and consults separately, which can push annual costs to $2,500-$5,400 depending on the provider and how comprehensive the lab panel is.
