How we ranked these

  1. Independent rating. Where available, we start from U.S. News & World Report's 2026–2027 GLP-1 telehealth platform ratings rather than a provider's self-reported reviews.
  2. Published pricing. We use the provider's own listed pricing, or figures independently reported by third-party comparison sites, and note when a price is a starting or promotional rate.
  3. Insurance & prior-authorization support. Programs with a dedicated prior-authorization team tend to secure coverage for brand-name medications faster than those without one.
  4. Clinical support model. We note whether coaching, lab monitoring, and specialist prescribers are included, since these vary widely between "fast and low-friction" and "high-touch clinical" programs.

Frequently asked questions

Is compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide the same as the brand-name drug?

Compounded GLP-1s contain the same active ingredient but are prepared by a compounding pharmacy rather than the original manufacturer, typically at a lower cash price. Availability has shifted as FDA drug-shortage determinations change — confirm current status directly with a provider before enrolling.

Will my insurance cover a GLP-1 prescribed through telehealth?

Sometimes. Coverage depends on your plan and diagnosis, which is why prior-authorization support (offered by Found, Ro, LifeMD, and WeightWatchers Clinic above) can matter more than the sticker price for insured patients.

How often is this page updated?

GLP-1 pricing and provider lineups change frequently. This page was last reviewed in August 2026; check the provider's own site for current pricing before enrolling.
