Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect. We don't ask you to create an account or submit health information on this site. We use Google Analytics to understand how pages are used (pages viewed, links clicked, device and approximate location), which uses cookies. We also use the Taboola pixel, which records page visits so Taboola can measure and target its ads; you can opt out of Taboola's interest-based ads through its own privacy settings.

Ad and affiliate tracking. If you arrive from an ad, the campaign and click identifiers in the link (such as utm parameters or a click ID) are passed along when you click through to a featured program, so the ad platform and affiliate network can attribute the visit. We keep a log of page views and clicks (with those identifiers, the page, and your approximate country) to measure which ads and pages work. We don't attach your name, email, or health information to these identifiers.

Third-party sites. Featured programs have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave this site. Any health information you provide is collected by that program, not by us.

Your choices. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.
