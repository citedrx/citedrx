Common questions

Why is Found ranked #1?

It's the only program in this chart that combines brand-name options, insurance prior-authorization help, and included coaching, and the only one with an independent rating (4.6/5 from U.S. News).

Which is cheapest?

Found has the lowest entry price ($49/mo membership), but medication is billed separately. Pallas Health's $139 first month includes compounded medication. Compare your total monthly cost at sign-up.

Do I need a prescription?

Yes. Every program here uses a licensed clinician to decide whether a GLP-1 is appropriate for you. GLP-1s are for adults with obesity, or overweight plus a weight-related condition, and aren't safe for everyone.
